Dental implants are small titanium posts that are gently placed into the jawbone to take the place of missing tooth roots. Once healed, they provide a secure foundation for fixed replacement teeth — whether a single crown, a bridge, or a full set — without the movement of a removable denture. If you’re missing a tooth, struggling with a loose denture, or hiding your smile, implants may be an option for you.

What do dental implants cost?

A single implant — including the implant, abutment and crown — starts from £2,500 at Essence Dental Clinic. Every mouth is different, so you’ll receive a personalised written quote after a consultation and digital scan, before committing to anything.

How treatment works

1 Consultation & scan. A relaxed chat about what you want, a digital scan where appropriate, and honest advice on whether implants are right for you — with a written summary and quote emailed to you.
2 Gentle placement. The implant is placed under local anaesthetic. Most patients are surprised how comfortable it is — and if you’re nervous, Dr Kothari will go at your pace.
3 Healing. Over the following months the implant bonds securely with your jawbone. You’ll have a personal timeline from your consultation.
4 Your new tooth. The final crown, bridge or fixed teeth are fitted — secure, natural-looking, and cared for like your own teeth.

Does getting an implant hurt?

Implants are placed gently under local anaesthetic, so the area is fully numb during treatment. Many patients are surprised how comfortable it is. Dr Kothari has particular experience with nervous patients and will go at your pace, explaining every step before it happens.

How long does treatment take?

Most treatments take a few months from placement to final tooth, so the implant can bond securely with the jawbone. You’ll get a personal timeline at your consultation based on your scan.

Are implants better than dentures?

Implants are fixed in place, so there’s none of the movement of a removable denture. They look, feel and function much like natural teeth, and can support a single crown, a bridge, or a full set of fixed teeth. Whether they’re right for you depends on your mouth — which is exactly what a consultation and scan will tell you.
