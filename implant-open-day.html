Implant Open Day Thu 3rd September

Thinking About Implants?
Come and Learn More

Dental implants are small titanium posts that are gently placed into the jawbone to take the place of missing tooth roots. Once healed, they provide a secure foundation for fixed replacement teeth — whether a single crown, a bridge, or a full set — that look, feel and function much like natural teeth, without the movement of a removable denture.

We’re holding an informal open day for patients who’d like to understand more about dental implants. It’s a relaxed chance to meet Dr Kothari, ask questions and find out whether implants might be an option for you. You can also read more about implant treatment and costs in Gants Hill before you visit.

What to expect on the day

A free-of-charge one-to-one chat with Dr Kothari about implant-fixed teeth
A digital scan to look at your teeth (where appropriate)
A written summary emailed to you
0% finance available Should you be eligible, you can discuss treatment options and any current implant offers with the team.
