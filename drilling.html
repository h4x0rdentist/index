Standing hazards

  • Motor direction: LR6 is all-CW; uppers alternate CW pilot → CCW Densah. Call the mode out loud at every bur change.
  • Reamer is length-specific: LR6 needs the B 6.6 conical reamer. If the tray only holds the classic 8/9.5/11/14/17 set, do not improvise with the B8 — skip the reamer and let the tap/implant do the coronal work, or re-plan. Check tonight.
  • Springback (uppers only): implant open before each site's final Densah bur — last bur out, implant in.
  • Stall past ~50 Ncm: back out, re-run final bur (LR6: reamer again at 15 rpm), re-place. Never muscle a cone connection.
  • Under 25 Ncm: cover screw and submerge. Sulcus formers: GH ≥ subcrestal + probed tissue, rounded up; wide profile at molars; two heights per site.
